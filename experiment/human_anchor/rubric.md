# Human Annotation Rubric: Big Five Personality Rating

## Instructions

You will read a group discussion transcript involving a "Candidate" and three AI agents (Alex, Jordan, Riley). Your task is to rate the **Candidate's** personality on the Big Five traits.

**Rate each trait on a 0.0 to 1.0 scale:**
- 0.0 = Very low (opposite of the trait description)
- 0.5 = Average / neutral
- 1.0 = Very high (strong display of the trait)

## Trait Definitions

### O — Openness to Experience
- **High (>0.7):** Proposes creative ideas, explores hypotheticals ("what if..."), engages with abstract concepts, welcomes novel approaches
- **Low (<0.3):** Sticks to practical/concrete points, dismisses novel ideas, prefers proven approaches
- **Anchor:** Does the candidate introduce ideas beyond what's required?

### C — Conscientiousness
- **High (>0.7):** Creates structure (timelines, priorities, action items), references earlier points, follows through on commitments, organized responses
- **Low (<0.3):** Scattered responses, forgets earlier points, no follow-through, disorganized
- **Anchor:** Does the candidate impose additional structure on the discussion?

### E — Extraversion
- **High (>0.7):** Long responses (>40 words/turn), initiates topics, elaborates beyond what's asked, engages others by name, asks questions
- **Low (<0.3):** Brief responses (<15 words/turn), waits to be addressed, minimal elaboration
- **Anchor:** How much does the candidate say relative to what's needed?

### A — Agreeableness
- **High (>0.7):** Accommodates others' views, acknowledges points ("good point"), rarely disagrees, seeks consensus
- **Low (<0.3):** Pushes back when challenged, defends position firmly, expresses direct disagreement
- **Anchor:** How does the candidate handle conflict and disagreement?

### N — Neuroticism
- **High (>0.7):** Shows stress signals (hedging, apologizing, self-doubt, seeking reassurance), defensive under pressure
- **Low (<0.3):** Calm language, stays focused, composed under pressure, confident tone
- **Anchor:** How does the candidate respond when challenged or under pressure?

## Important Notes

1. Only rate the **Candidate's** behavior, not the AI agents
2. Consider ABSENCE of behavior as evidence (e.g., zero disagreements = high A)
3. Base ratings on the **full transcript**, not just one or two turns
4. Do not let the discussion topic bias your rating — focus on HOW the candidate communicates, not WHAT they discuss
